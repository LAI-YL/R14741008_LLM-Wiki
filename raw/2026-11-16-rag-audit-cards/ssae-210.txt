Standard: AT-C Section 210 (SSAE)
Title (En): Review Engagements under SSAE
Title (Zh): 確信複核案件（有限確信規範）
Category: SSAE (AT-C)
Domain: 複核確信案件
Scope: 非歷史財務報表標的
Related PCAOB Standard: N/A

Summary (核心規範):
會計師提供『有限確信 (Limited Assurance / Negative Assurance)』。程序以向管理階層詢問（Inquiry）及分析性程序（Analytical Procedures）為主，不出具深入實質性測試與控制測試。

Exam Trap (考試陷阱):
⚠️ 報告用語辨析：結論以消極文字表述：『Based on our review, we are not aware of any material modifications that should be made...』。若需要合理確信，絕不能委任 Review！

TBS Tip (實務要點):
執行 SSAE Review 仍『必須具備獨立性』。此點與 SSARS Compilation 完全不同！

Keywords: 210, ssae review, limited assurance, negative assurance, we are not aware, 確信複核, 有限確信
